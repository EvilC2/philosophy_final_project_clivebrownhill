PHILOSOPHY QUIZ — REBALANCED 2.0 (WEIGHTED SCORING)

WHY THIS VERSION EXISTS
The earlier "perfectly balanced" version was fair but low-resolution: every
answer gave +1 to 3 philosophies, so results landed in a near-tie and didn't
reflect what people actually identify with. 2.0 fixes that with WEIGHTED answers
while keeping the math fair.

HOW SCORING WORKS NOW
- Each answer still has 3 philosophy tags.
- One tag is the answer's LEAD (the philosophy it most expresses). The lead
  scores 3 points. The other two tags score 1 each.
- Some answers are intentionally leadless: all three tags score 1.
- The final option on every question is "none of these / not sure". It has no
  tags and scores nothing.

WHY THE MATH STILL WORKS (no philosophy is favoured)
- Every philosophy is the LEAD on exactly 7 answers across the quiz.
- Every philosophy appears as a tag exactly 22 times.
- So every philosophy has the same maximum points available: 22 + 7x2 = 36.
- The weights only sharpen the result. They do not tilt it toward anyone.

WHAT THE LEAD DOES FOR YOU
If your answers lean a certain way, that philosophy's lead points stack up fast
and it rises clearly to the top instead of tying with everything else. Tested:
a Maslow/Stoic/Existentialist set of answers returns exactly those three on top,
well clear of the rest.
