PHILOSOPHY QUIZ — REBALANCED VERSION (15 PHILOSOPHIES) — REVISION 3

WHAT STAYED THE SAME (the balance, do not revert)
- 15 philosophies.
- Every scoring option carries exactly 3 philosophy tags.
- Every question splits the 15 philosophies into 5 options x 3 tags, so each
  philosophy appears once per question and an equal number of times across the
  whole quiz (22 appearances each).

NEW IN REVISION 3: WEIGHTED LEAD (sharper results, still fair)
- Each option now has a LEAD: its single dominant philosophy. The lead gets +2,
  its two bundle-mates get +1 each. A strong pick now points more clearly at
  one philosophy instead of splitting evenly three ways.
- To keep the math fair, every philosophy is the lead the EXACT same number of
  times: 7 leads each. (5 options across the quiz are left flat, all +1, to make
  the count come out even.)
- So the TOTAL points available per philosophy are still identical: 29 each
  (22 tag points + 7 lead points). No philosophy is favoured by the math. The
  lead weight only sharpens the result, it does not tilt the base rates.
- Why this was added: the old equal +1 scoring was fair but blurry. Picking a
  "stoic" answer gave its two bundle-mates the exact same boost, so a person's
  real top philosophies kept getting watered down and the result could miss
  them. The lead bonus fixes that while staying perfectly balanced. Verified by
  script: lead counts 7/7 across all 15, total weight 29/29.

ALSO CHANGED THIS REVISION (wording feedback)
- Q1 reframed to "an evening you'd look back on."
- Q4 option A rewritten as the self-actualisation / "fullest version of myself"
  answer (this is the maslow option you said was missing).
- Q7 option D now mentions growing into your potential AND leaving a legacy,
  without outshining the other options.
- Q21 (2am thought) options A, B, D, E rewritten to be realistic late-night
  thoughts instead of lines no one actually thinks.

STILL IN PLACE FROM REVISION 2
- "Not sure / none of these fit" on every question, with NO tags and no lead, so
  it scores nothing and never touches the balance.
- Em dashes cut almost everywhere so the wording reads human and clear.
- Q12 is about YOUR OWN suffering only, not someone else's.

Note on each option below: the philosophy marked (LEAD +2) is the one that gets
the extra point. Five options are marked (flat) and weight all three equally.
