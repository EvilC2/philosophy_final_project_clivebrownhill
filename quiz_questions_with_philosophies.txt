Q1: A perfect special evening — celebration, date, something you'd actually look forward to.

A) Somewhere worth being. Good food, good drink, people I actually like, no phones.

Philosophies: epicurean, existentialist, hooks

C) Something I've never done before. Novelty, aliveness, the feeling of being awake to life.

D) Alone and completely off the hook. Reading, making something, existing without performing.

Philosophies: stoic, confucian

B) Diogenes — rejected everything society offered and seemed genuinely unbothered by all of it.
Philosophies: cynic, nihilist

C) bell hooks — built her entire life around love and community as serious intellectual and ethical practice.
Philosophies: hooks, watsuji

D) Simone de Beauvoir — walked away from every role assigned to her and defined her own existence entirely on her own terms.
Philosophies: existentialist, nussbaum

E) Siddhartha Gautama — left total security and comfort behind to understand why people suffer and whether it could end.
Philosophies: buddhist, epicurean

F) Confucius — spent his life trying to convince powerful people to be good, mostly failed, and kept going anyway.
Philosophies: confucian, christian
